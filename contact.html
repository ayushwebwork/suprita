Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our manpower services for government clients.

How quickly can you deploy staff?

We can typically begin deployment within 7–14 days of contract signing. Emergency security deployments can be arranged within 24–48 hours.

Are you registered for government tenders?

Yes, we are GeM (Government e-Marketplace) registered with PAN, GST, CLRA license, PSARA license, and ISO certification ready for GFR compliance.

Is your staff insured and documented?

Absolutely. All deployed staff have active PF and ESI coverage, are police-verified, and carry proper identity documentation for audits.

What is the minimum contract duration?

We work with both short-term project-based and long-term annual contracts. Minimum duration is typically 3 months, with exceptions for government projects.

Do you handle replacement of absent staff?

Yes, we maintain a backup pool and guarantee same-day replacement of absent staff for all government deployments.

Which regions do you serve?

We currently operate across Delhi NCR (Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad, Ghaziabad) and are expanding to more cities across North India.

Suprita Sales & Services Pvt. Ltd. is a government-approved, ISO certified manpower and facility management agency headquartered in New Delhi, India.
